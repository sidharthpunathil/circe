Your skills are on your machine, not here

This registry lives in the cloud, so it can’t reach {{ .Source }} on your laptop. Send them up from your terminal instead. Two commands and you’re done.

First, connect

skillbuilder login {{ .BaseURL }}

Then send one skill

skillbuilder push ~/.claude/skills/my-skill

Or everything at once

skillbuilder push ~/.claude/skills

Point it at one skill or a whole folder of them and it works out which. Everything that belongs to a skill comes along, and nothing you already have gets overwritten. Or try a repo and a share code, which both work from anywhere.
